Questions & answers.

What is Collaj?

Collaj is an iPhone app for collages that mix photos and videos. Pick a grid or place things freely, trim, loop and change the speed of each video clip, then export a photo, an MP4 video or a GIF, up to 4K.

Can I put photos and videos in the same collage?

Yes, that’s what Collaj is for. Each video plays in its own cell, with its own trim, loop and speed.

Is Collaj free?

Free to start. Photo collages are free, with no ads. Your first video export is free; after that, each video export costs a short ad, or you go Premium.

Premium removes the ads and adds several videos in one collage (your first one is on us, once), 4K and 60 fps export and the full creative library. It’s a weekly, monthly or yearly subscription (the yearly plan includes a free trial), or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Is there a watermark?

No. Not on photos, videos or GIFs.

Do I need an account? Where do my photos go?

No account, no sign-up. Collages are made and exported on your iPhone. Your photos and videos are never uploaded.

Does it work without internet?

Making and exporting collages happens on the iPhone. Ads, purchases, restoring Premium and photos or videos stored only in iCloud need a connection.

Which languages?

English and French, plus Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, German and Japanese in beta. Collaj follows your iPhone’s language, and you can pick another one in its settings.
